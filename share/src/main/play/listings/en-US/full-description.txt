Send files to nearby phones over Wi-Fi, Quick Share compatible

Features:
- Exchanges files with stock Android devices over the real Quick Share protocol
- End-to-end encrypted transfers with a UKEY2 handshake
- Send straight from the system share sheet
- Receive with the app closed, toggled from a Quick Settings tile
- Received files stay app-private until you choose to save or share them
- Files go directly device to device and never touch a server

100% offline
