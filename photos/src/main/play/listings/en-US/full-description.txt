A photos app with a full editor and on-device AI search

Features:
- Browse your gallery with an adjustable grid
- Non-destructive editor: curves, HSL, levels, healing, liquify, layers and more
- Search by what is in a photo, by text in it, and by who is in it
- Faces grouped into people, all computed on device
- Browse photos on a map
- Biometric-locked secure folder
- Wallpaper, live wallpaper, and a home screen widget

Internet only used for: the basemap tiles behind the photos-on-a-map view
