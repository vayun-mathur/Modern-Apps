A home screen replacement with real widget support, plus a TV mode

Features:
- Paged icon grid, a hotseat, folders, and a searchable app drawer
- Widgets hosted directly, including providers that need configuring first
- Cell grid whose size you choose
- Layout kept in an encrypted database on your device
- On Android TV and Google TV it becomes a calm banner home: 16:9 app tiles, HDMI inputs, clock header, D-pad reorder
- No account, nothing collected, nothing uploaded

100% offline
