Receive a mirrored phone screen on your TV

Features:
- Install on an Android TV or Google TV and it appears in the Cast app on a phone
- Pair once with a six-digit code, then reconnect silently
- Video decoded in hardware and letterboxed to fit the panel
- Play cast audio and video with a Now Playing screen and transport controls
- No account and no server - it only talks to phones on your local network

100% offline
