A picture logic puzzle, with a new one every day

Features:
- Fill the grid from the number clues to reveal the hidden picture
- One level open at a time, plus a daily puzzle shared by everyone that date
- Puzzles are generated on device and checked to be solvable by reasoning alone
- Switch between filling and crossing out, long press for the other one
- Three hearts per board, and an empty cell you fill costs you one

100% offline
