A voxel sandbox game with a full Rust + Vulkan engine

Features:
- Infinite procedurally generated world with 25 biomes and villages
- Native Rust engine on a hand-written Vulkan backend
- Block placement and destruction, crafting, fishing, and villager trading
- World saves, achievements, and 19 languages
- Optional shared online worlds, end-to-end encrypted through an untrusted relay

Internet only used for: optional online multiplayer worlds
