A free, open-source education app for kids and curious learners

Features:
- K2 to K12 curriculum with quizzes, tracing, and drag-match activities
- Separate age-banded flows for young children and older explorers
- Curriculum is bundled in the app, with progress tracked on device
- Parent gate and daily goal tracking
- Badges and streaks to keep kids motivated
- Video lessons streamed from Khan Academy / YouTube

Internet only used for: streaming the video lessons
