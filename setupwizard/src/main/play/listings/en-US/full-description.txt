The first-run setup wizard for Modern Apps OS

Features:
- Guides first boot: language, Wi-Fi, date and time, location, lock screen and restore
- Warns when the bootloader is unlocked, and offers to turn OEM unlocking back off
- Hands each step to the app that owns it rather than reimplementing it
- Runs a shorter flow for a secondary user, who is setting up a profile, not a device
- Disables itself the moment setup is finished

100% offline
