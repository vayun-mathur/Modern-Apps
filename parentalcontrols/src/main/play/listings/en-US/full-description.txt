Enables Android's built-in parental controls with an on-device PIN

Features:
- Turns on the screen time, bedtime schedule and app limit controls built into the system
- Filters mature content in browsers and search, using the system's own web content filters
- Restricts what can be installed, through the system app store filters
- Protected by a PIN you set on the device, with no account to create and nothing to sign in to
- Runs entirely on the device; no usage data leaves it and nothing is uploaded

100% offline
