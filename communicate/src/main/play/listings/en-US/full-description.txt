A dialer and messaging app with WhatsApp, Signal, and Google Voice

Features:
- Works as your default phone app and your default SMS/MMS app
- Native WhatsApp and Signal clients, not web bridges
- Google Voice calls and texts, appearing as real system calls
- Unified inbox across every backend
- VoIP calls with screen sharing
- T9 dialing, call logs, polls, and shared contacts

Requires internet
