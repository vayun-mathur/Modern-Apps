Read the device log and system error reports, entirely on device

Features:
- View the system log or a single app's log, with the newest lines first on screen
- Filter by log level, by ring buffer, or with a regular expression
- Read crash, ANR and battery reports sent by the system, including native tombstones
- Pinch to resize the text, and attach a description before sharing
- Copy to the clipboard, save to a file, or share without writing the log to storage
- Jump straight from a crash report to the log of the app that crashed

100% offline
