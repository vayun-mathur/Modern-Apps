Discover UCP merchants and review shopping checkouts

Features:
- Search compatible UCP merchants via Shopify Global Catalog, merchant domain entry, or the public UCPList directory
- Validate each merchant's live advertised profile before showing products; directory badges are leads, not access grants
- Refresh product details to confirm the exact variant before adding integer-quantity items to the cart
- Prepare checkout sessions with per-merchant totals plus authoritative checkout totals and legal links
- Pending sessions and cart lines survive browser handoffs and partial failures without confirming a paid order
- Native payment completion stays disabled without a supported handler, provider approval, and authorized merchant access

Requires internet
