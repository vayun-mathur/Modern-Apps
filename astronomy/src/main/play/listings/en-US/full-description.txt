An AR night sky viewer for planets, stars, and constellations

Features:
- Real-time sky map that follows where you point the phone, with a red night mode
- Camera passthrough so the sky lines up with what you can actually see
- Time scrubber to simulate the sky at any moment, or snap back to live
- Constellation lines and artwork, deep-sky objects, and a coordinate grid
- Search any object for its rise, set, and transit times
- Star and planet catalogs are bundled in the app, not downloaded

100% offline
