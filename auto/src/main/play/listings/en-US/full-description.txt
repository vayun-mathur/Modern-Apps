Projects a driving-safe interface to your car's Android Auto display

Features:
- Connects over USB or wirelessly to head units that support Android Auto
- Mirrors navigation, media and calls to the car screen at the resolution it asks for
- Takes touch, rotary and steering-wheel input from the car and drives the phone with it
- Sends speed, gear, night mode and the car's other sensors to the apps that need them
- Runs entirely on the device; the car connection is direct and nothing is uploaded

100% offline
