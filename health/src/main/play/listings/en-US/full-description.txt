A health metric manager meant to replace Google Fit

Features:
- Activity, vitals, body composition, exercise, sleep, hydration and nutrition
- Vaccination and medication records, stored as FHIR in Health Connect
- Attach a PDF or photo of a vaccination card to any immunisation
- Vaccines and medications filled in from bundled CVX and RxNorm code sets
- Backed by Health Connect, so it works with whatever already records your data
- Charts and history per metric
- AI insights generated by the on-device model in MA OpenAssistant
- Export your data to a file
- No account and no cloud sync

100% offline
