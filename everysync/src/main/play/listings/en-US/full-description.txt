A free, open-source universal sync hub for your personal data

Features:
- Sync contacts and calendars across Google, iCloud, Nextcloud, and custom CalDAV
- Works with any CardDAV server too
- Health Connect bridge for fitness data
- Background periodic sync via WorkManager
- Credentials kept in app-private encrypted storage
- OAuth2 PKCE flow via Custom Tabs (no client secrets shipped)

Requires internet
