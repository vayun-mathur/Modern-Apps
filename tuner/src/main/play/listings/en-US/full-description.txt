A precise chromatic tuner for guitar, ukulele, and piano

Features:
- Note tab with a sub-cent tuning meter and the measured frequency in Hz
- Adjustable reference pitch from 415 to 466 Hz, for baroque, 432, and orchestral A
- Tells you which string you are tuning, and warns when it is the wrong one
- Reads the raw microphone feed, so low strings are measured, not filtered away
- Says when it is unsure rather than guessing
- Chord detection is not built yet
- No network permission at all
