A news reader that fetches articles directly from publishers - no account, no server of ours

Features:
- News from built-in sources (BBC, The Guardian, Al Jazeera, TechCrunch, ProPublica, El País, NPR, DW, Ars Technica, The Verge, Hacker News), each fetched from its own sitemap or feed
- Enable or disable sources; filter the merged, newest-first feed by source
- Clean reader view extracted on your device (no tracking, no external service), with adjustable text size and serif/sans choice
- Toggle to the original web page, or open the article in your browser
- Save articles for later; feed and read articles are cached for offline reading

Internet only used for: fetching publisher sitemaps, articles and article images directly from the publisher sites
