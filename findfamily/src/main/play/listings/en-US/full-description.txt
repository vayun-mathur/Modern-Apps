An end-to-end encrypted location sharing app for family and friends

Features:
- End-to-end encryption
- No emails, phone numbers, or any other personal information required
- Temporarily turn location sharing on or off with specific people
- Saved places, with notifications when people enter or leave them
- See battery status, current speed, and how long someone has been at a place
- UWB precision ranging where the hardware supports it
- Quick Settings tile to stop sharing instantly

Requires internet
